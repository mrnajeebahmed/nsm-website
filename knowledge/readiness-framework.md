# NSM AI Readiness Framework (oAIRI and Decision Engine)

## oAIRI organizational readiness assessment
The NSM AI Readiness Hub uses the oAIRI v3.2 framework (adapted from airi.foundation, CC BY 4.0). It has 15 questions across five pillars: P1 Leadership & Culture, P2 Ethics & Governance, P3 Business Value, P4 Data Foundation, P5 Infrastructure & Standards. Each answer scores 0 to 5 and the overall score is the mean of all 15 answers. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Maturity levels
Scores map to five levels: L0 AI Unaware (below 1), L1 AI Aware (1 to 2), L2 AI Ready (2 to 3), L3 AI Competent (3 to 4) and L4 AI Catalyst (4 and above). L2 AI Ready is the success target for most growing businesses, not expert status.

## Ethics gate
Ethics & Governance (P2) is a gate. A P2 level of L0 is STOP, L1 is CONDITIONS, and L2 or higher is GO. An organization's overall level can never be higher than its P2 level. When the gate is STOP, NSM recommends a governance-first AI Opportunity Sprint before any build: AI guidelines, risk controls and an approved-tools list.

## Project Decision Engine
The AIRI Decision Engine v3.0 scores one candidate AI project. Feasibility = 0.7 × Data (P4) + 0.3 × Infrastructure (P5). Value adds Business Value (P3). People adds 2 × Leadership & Culture (P1), because projects fail on people more than technology. The total is multiplied by the ethics gate (0, 0.5 or 1) and divided by a maximum of 20. Bands: 75%+ Strong, 50–75% Moderate, 25–50% Weak, under 25% Very low, and 0 when ethics-killed. The lowest pillar is the bottleneck to fix first. Projects are ranked on a shortlist and the top five go to leadership.

## Automation tracks
Organizations at L0–L1 start on Track A, automation: use AI to do a specific task for a process and reduce a pain metric. Organizations at L2 and above can pursue Track B, transformation: AI agents handle production work so people focus on judgment and strategy.
