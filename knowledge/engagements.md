# Engagement Models & Pricing Bands

## AI Opportunity Sprint (2–3 weeks)
Deep-dive process audit, data readiness check and a working proof-of-concept for the client's #1 workflow. Indicative investment: USD 8,000–15,000. Ideal first step for most growing businesses.

## Production Build & Integration (6–10 weeks)
Full end-to-end engineering, testing, security hardening and deployment of a custom AI system into daily operations. Indicative investment: USD 35,000–80,000 depending on integrations and scope.

## Managed AI Operations & Advisory (ongoing)
Continuous model monitoring, prompt and pipeline optimization, cost management and quarterly expansion sprints. Priced as a monthly retainer scoped to the number of production workflows.
