# NSM Technologies Services

## AI Strategy & Opportunity Mapping
NSM runs executive workshops, process bottleneck audits, feasibility scoring and governance frameworks. The deliverable is a prioritized 90-day execution blueprint ranked by payback period and technical effort. Best for businesses unsure where AI fits or with many competing ideas.

## Workflow & Process Automation
NSM automates multi-step business processes such as lead qualification, invoice reconciliation, customer onboarding and ticket triage, always with human-in-the-loop safeguards for high-risk steps. Pipelines are integrated directly into the tools a client already uses (CRM, ERP, helpdesk, email, WhatsApp).

## Custom AI Copilots, Agents & Knowledge Systems (RAG)
NSM builds secure, hallucination-checked LLM applications grounded in a client's proprietary data using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), plus multi-agent task runners. Examples: internal SOP copilots, customer-facing policy concierges, sales assistants. Answers include citations and accuracy is measured with evaluation suites before launch.

## Data & Systems Integration
NSM connects CRM, ERP, finance and communication tools into a unified, AI-ready data foundation with clean data governance, master records, audit logs and access controls. This is often the prerequisite for reliable AI agents.

## Digital Product & Venture Engineering
NSM architects, prototypes and launches AI-native digital products and modernizes legacy customer portals using full-stack, cloud-native engineering.
