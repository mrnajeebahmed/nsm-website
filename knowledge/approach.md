# How NSM Works

## Delivery lifecycle
NSM follows six steps: 1 Discover (identify high-ROI bottlenecks), 2 Architect (secure, model-agnostic blueprint), 3 Prototype (validate with real business data in weeks), 4 Integrate (connect CRM, ERP, APIs), 5 Govern (guardrails, accuracy evaluations, human-in-the-loop), 6 Operate & Scale (handover, team training, continuous optimization).

## Ownership and no vendor lock-in
Clients own 100% of the code, agents, prompts, data and playbooks. Systems are deployed into the client's cloud account (AWS, Azure or GCP) where possible. Because the architecture is model-agnostic, clients can switch between OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini or open-weight models such as Llama without a rewrite.

## Security and data privacy
NSM uses enterprise API agreements under which providers do not train on client data, encrypts data in transit and at rest, applies role-based access control and SSO, logs AI decisions for auditability, and can deploy private or open-weight models for sensitive workloads. Human-in-the-loop review is used for regulated or high-impact decisions.

## Timelines
Most proofs-of-concept are delivered in 2–3 weeks. Production systems typically go live in 6–10 weeks.
