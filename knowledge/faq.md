# Frequently Asked Questions

## Who does NSM work with?
Growing businesses and mid-market enterprises in e-commerce and retail, professional services, healthcare, logistics and manufacturing, SaaS and technology, and financial services that want measurable operational impact from AI rather than experiments.

## Which AI models does NSM use?
NSM is model-agnostic: OpenAI GPT models, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini and Meta Llama, chosen per use case for accuracy, cost, latency and data-residency needs. Orchestration uses LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Python/FastAPI, n8n and Make. Data platforms include PostgreSQL with pgvector, Pinecone and Supabase on AWS or Azure.

## Do we need clean data before starting?
No. The AI Opportunity Sprint includes a data readiness check. Many first projects, such as intelligent document processing, actually create structured data from unstructured documents.

## What ROI can we expect?
Typical results: 5x faster speed-to-lead with automated lead intake, 80% less manual data entry with document processing, 60–75% ticket deflection with a grounded customer concierge, and new-hire ramp time cut in half with an internal SOP copilot. Most production builds target payback within 3–9 months.

## How do we get started?
Take the 3-minute AI Readiness Assessment or the ROI calculator on the website, then book a free 20-minute strategy call with an NSM architect.
